# The Panel Guide

A free, plain-language guide for therapists who work through Headway, Alma, Rula or a
similar platform and are thinking about contracting with insurance plans directly.

Live at **https://thepanelguide.org**. Made by the team at [Pablo](https://pablo.health).

The whole site is one file, `index.html`, served by GitHub Pages.

Spot something wrong or out of date? Email guide@pablo.health.

This is general information, not legal, tax or billing advice.
